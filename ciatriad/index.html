Classify the Incident

You'll read 12 short situations pulled from real provincial-government operations. For each one, decide which part of security it breaks — Confidentiality, Integrity, or Availability. Some break more than one.

Confidentiality

Information was seen, taken, or leaked by someone who shouldn't have had it.

Integrity

Information or a record was changed, and can no longer be trusted as accurate.

Availability

A system or service that should be usable isn't — for anyone who needs it.
