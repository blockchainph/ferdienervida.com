Module 4: Security Best Practices Audit

You are the auditor. A small city office has a fake permit website. Look for what is unsafe, fix who can see what, then write a short report. You find weaknesses by looking, not by hacking.

Practice only here. Probing a real website without permission is illegal (RA 10175, illegal access). RA 10175 is the Cybercrime Prevention Act. Illegal access means getting into a system you were not allowed to enter.

What you will do
