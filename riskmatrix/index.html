Rate It, Then Respond

You'll read 8 real situations from provincial-government operations. For each one: rate how likely it is, rate how bad it would be, then decide the right response — one of these four:

TR
Treat

Reduce the likelihood or impact directly — fix the actual weakness.

TF
Transfer

Shift the risk to someone else — a vendor contract, insurance, an SLA.

TL
Tolerate

Accept it and monitor — not worth the cost or effort to fix.

TE
Terminate

Eliminate it entirely by stopping the activity that causes it.
