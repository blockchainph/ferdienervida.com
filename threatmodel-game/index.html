          Your own office

          Think about the office where you work. Pick the one asset (a system, data, a device, or a person) you would most want to protect.

          Paragraph 1: Who or what could threaten it, and how could they get in? How likely is it, and how badly would it hurt?

          Paragraph 2: What is the first practical fix you would propose, and who in your office would you need to work with to make it happen?

          Keep it general. Don't include passwords, the real names of staff, or exact weaknesses someone could misuse. Your file will be sent by email.
