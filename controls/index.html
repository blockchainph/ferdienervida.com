Module 3: Configure an Office

Put twelve security controls where they belong, then find the one gap that is left. This is a practice activity and it is not graded.

City Treasurer's Office (practice)

This fictional office has: a payroll database, 40 staff laptops, citizen records on paper, a vendor with remote access, one shared admin password, and backups that were never tested.

Every name and detail here is invented.
