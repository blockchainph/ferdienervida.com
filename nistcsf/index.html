Sort by Function

You'll read 8 short situations from real provincial-government operations. For each one, decide which of the six NIST CSF 2.0 Functions it belongs to. Some belong to more than one.

Govern

Strategy, policy, and who's responsible.

Identify

Understanding your current risks.

Protect

Safeguards that manage risk.

Detect

Finding and analyzing an attack.

Respond

Acting on a detected incident.

Recover

Restoring what the incident affected.
